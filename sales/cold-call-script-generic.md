# ArgusReach — Generic Cold Call Script

## Opening (when they answer)

"Hey — is this [name]?"

*(they confirm)*

"Hey [name] — this is Vito, I'm calling from ArgusReach. I actually sent you a couple emails over the past few weeks — didn't want it to get buried. Do you have literally two minutes?"

---

## If they say yes

"So basically — we help [type of business] build a consistent pipeline by handling all the outreach for you. We identify your ideal prospects, reach out in your name by email and follow up by phone, and anyone interested gets routed straight to your calendar. You don't have to do any of the cold outreach yourself — we run the whole thing.

Would it make sense to grab 15 minutes to see if it's a fit for [their company]?"

---

## If they ask "how does it work?"

"We build a targeted list of your ideal prospects — whatever criteria makes sense for your business — write personalized emails that go out in your name, and follow up automatically. You just show up to the meetings. Fully managed, nothing falls on you."

---

## If they say "we already do that" or "we have someone"

"Totally fair — out of curiosity, is it getting you consistent results? A lot of people we talk to have something in place but it's not running consistently. If it's working well, stick with it — but if you ever want a second opinion I'm happy to show you what we do."

---

## If they say "send me some info"

"Absolutely — what's the best email? I'll send over our site and a page specific to your industry — and feel free to grab time on my calendar if anything looks interesting."

---

## If they say "not interested"

"No worries at all — appreciate you picking up. Have a great day."

---

## Close

"Great — I'll send over a quick note with my calendar link. Takes 15 minutes and I'll show you exactly what this looks like for [their business]. Sound good?"

---

## Key Rules
- Never pitch more than 2 sentences before asking for their time
- If they push back, ask a question — don't keep selling
- Energy: warm, direct, not salesy. Checking if it's a fit, not closing on the phone
- Goal of the call: get 15 minutes, not explain everything
